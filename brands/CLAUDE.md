# brands/ - rules of engagement

Output that isn't Charlie's journalism: the satire account and quizzes. This is where Claude
drafts freely, because Charlie picks rather than writes. Volume is fine here; quality is set
by what he keeps.

| Path | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `satire/` | Headline batches, the house voice, what's been posted |
| `quiz/` | Quiz batches with answers and sources, what's been used |

## Rules

- **Satire never appears under Charlie's name** and is labelled as satire wherever it's posted.
- **No invented quotes a real person could be believed to have said**, and nothing that reads
  as a factual allegation about a named person. Mock the situation, not a fabricated act.
- **Every quiz answer has a source** next to it. A wrong answer costs more trust than ten
  right ones earn.
- Keep a `posted.md` / `used.md` log in each so nothing is reused by accident.
- Batches are dated files: `YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Don't pre-generate beyond the next week.
