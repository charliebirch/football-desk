# Satire house voice

Brand: **Whodle** (whodle.io, hello@whodle.io). Profile picture and palette in `logos/`.
Model: The Betoota Advocate and The Daily Mash, applied to English
football. Deadpan news register, absurd premise, true-to-life detail.

## What works

- The headline does the joke; the first line plays it completely straight.
- Specific beats general: a real ground, a real fixture, a real kind of fan.

## Off limits

- See `brands/CLAUDE.md`: no fake quotes from real people, no factual-sounding allegations.
- Tragedies, injuries, abuse, anything involving real children.
