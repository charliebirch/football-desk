# Pitch a story

**May create/edit:** `stories/<slug>/pitch.md`, `outlets/<outlet>.md` (research, pitch
history). **Never:** sends anything. A Gmail draft only if Charlie asks for one. **Stops at:**
the pitch text for Charlie to edit and send himself.

1. The story should be `ready` (or Charlie says pitch it as an idea before drafting, which is
   normal for features; say which you're doing).
2. Read the outlet file. If there isn't one, research the outlet first (recent pieces in this
   area, editor, rates, guidelines; each with a source and date) and create it.
3. Draft in `pitch.md`:
   - Subject: `PITCH: <proposed headline>`
   - Paragraph 1: the story and why now.
   - Paragraph 2: what the reader gets and what makes it Charlie's (the seat from Australia,
     the data, the access).
   - One line: length, timing, and a link to his best relevant published piece.
   Under 200 words. Charlie edits it into his own words; editors are screening for AI-written
   pitches.
4. Add a row to the outlet's `## Pitch history` with status `drafted`. Charlie updates it to
   `sent`; a follow-up is due a week later if there's no reply.
5. Set the story to `status: pitched` only once Charlie says he's sent it.
