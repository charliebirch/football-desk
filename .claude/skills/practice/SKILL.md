# Practice drill

**May create/edit:** `foundation/practice/YYYY-MM-DD-<drill>.md`. **May copy** a finished piece
into `foundation/voice-samples/` only when Charlie says it's good enough. **Stops at:** the
brief (when setting) or the feedback (when reviewing).

## Setting a drill

Read `foundation/craft.md` and the latest `foundation/taste/` files. Pick one technique to
practise and one real football moment as material (this week's matches are best). The brief:
the technique, the moment, a length (200-500 words), a time limit, and one taste piece that
shows the technique done well. Examples:

- Same match, three ways: straight report, opinion, the far-away fan at 3am.
- Five openings for one piece; pick the best and say why.
- 300 words on one moment, no stats allowed. Then 300 with one stat doing the work.
- Rewrite a dull club statement as a column.

## Giving feedback

Charlie writes the drill himself. Then, in the same file:

1. What worked, specifically (quote his own line back).
2. The one change that would improve it most, and why, linked to a technique in `craft.md`.
3. Up to three line-level notes. Point and explain; don't rewrite his paragraphs.
4. A next drill that builds on this one.

Be honest. Praise that isn't specific doesn't help him improve.
