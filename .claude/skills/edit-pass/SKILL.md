# Edit pass

**May create:** `stories/<slug>/edit-notes.md`. **May edit:** `draft.md` only if Charlie asks
you to apply specific changes. **Stops at:** the notes, ranked, for Charlie to accept or
reject.

Read first: `foundation/style-guide.md`, `foundation/red-lines.md`, the story's `brief.md`
and `sources.md`, then `draft.md`.

## Order of the notes, most important first

1. **Does it do what the brief says?** If the angle drifted, say so before anything else.
2. **Structure:** does the opening earn the second paragraph; is anything in the wrong order;
   what could go.
3. **Red lines:** any quote or fact with no row in `sources.md`; anything that could be unfair
   to a named person.
4. **Voice:** places that don't sound like Charlie per the style guide, including anything that
   reads as AI-written (stock phrasing, tidy triplets, hedged conclusions).
5. **Lines:** specific cuts and rewrites, each with the original, the suggestion, and why.
6. **Headline and standfirst:** three options, different in approach, not wording.

Aim for a 10-20% cut unless the brief says otherwise. Suggest; don't rewrite his paragraphs
wholesale. Where a passage needs rewriting, say what's wrong and let him do it.

When Charlie accepts notes and asks you to apply them, apply exactly those, then move the
story to `status: checking`.
