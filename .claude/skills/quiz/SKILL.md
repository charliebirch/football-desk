# Quiz batch

**May create:** `brands/quiz/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. **Stops at:** the quiz with answers and sources,
for Charlie to check.

1. Read `brands/quiz/used.md` (create it if missing) so nothing repeats.
2. Ten questions unless Charlie says otherwise: a mix of easy, medium and one hard. At least
   two from the week's matches; one FPL; one from the far-away-fan angle when it fits.
3. Every answer gets a source URL beside it, opened and checked today. Multiple-choice
   distractors must be plausible and definitely wrong.
4. Flag any question whose answer could change before publication (a record that could be
   broken this weekend).
5. Don't touch the Whodle repo; Whodle has its own clue process. A quiz can link to Whodle.
