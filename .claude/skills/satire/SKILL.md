# Satire batch

**May create:** `brands/satire/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. **Stops at:** the batch, for Charlie to pick.

1. Read `brands/satire/voice.md` (especially the calibration set) and `brands/CLAUDE.md`.
2. Get this week's real football news from the web. The joke has to land on something people
   are actually talking about.
3. Write 20 headlines, each with a one-line straight-faced first sentence. Vary the target:
   clubs, the league, broadcasters, fans, FPL, the far-away fan.
4. Check every line against the off-limits list. Drop anything that puts invented words in a
   real person's mouth or could read as a real allegation.
5. Mark your own top five. Charlie picks; his picks go into `voice.md`'s calibration set and
   `posted.md` once posted.
