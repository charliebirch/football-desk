# Content pillars

Every idea belongs to exactly one. Frontmatter field `pillar:` takes the slug.

| Slug | Pillar | Examples | Main destinations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `big-questions` | Big English-football questions | Were goals better before VAR? Are City fans in denial? How much does football matter to the English? | UK features desks, newsletter |
| `far-away-fan` | The fan watching from Australia | 3am kick-offs, staying connected, Sydney supporters' clubs | Australian outlets, newsletter |
| `fpl` | FPL and FPL Draft | Draft strategy, data-led player calls | Newsletter, FPL sites |
| `humour-games` | Satire, quizzes, Whodle | Satire headlines, weekly quiz | Satire account, newsletter quiz slot, Whodle |

An idea that fits none of these is either a new pillar (a strategy call for `STATUS.md`) or
not for this desk.
