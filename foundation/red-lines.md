# Red lines

These hold whatever the deadline. A piece that breaks one doesn't ship.

1. **No quote that wasn't heard, recorded or read in a primary source.** AI never supplies a
   quote, and a paraphrase is never dressed up as a quote.
2. **Every stat traces to a named source Charlie or Claude opened**, recorded in the story's
   `sources.md` with the URL and the date it was checked. No stat from a chatbot's memory.
3. **Charlie's byline means Charlie's prose.** AI edits, challenges and restructures; it doesn't
   ghost-write.
4. **Ask each outlet about its AI policy when commissioned, and disclose significant use.**
   Record the answer in `outlets/<outlet>.md`.
5. **Embargoed, off-the-record or private material never goes into a tool that trains on it.**
   Check the tool's training setting first; if unsure, don't upload.
6. **Satire is labelled as satire everywhere it appears** and never invents a quote a real
   person could be believed to have said.
7. **Anything that could be defamatory gets a second look before it goes out**: claims about
   named people's motives, conduct or finances need a source and fair wording. When in doubt,
   flag it to Charlie; Claude is not a lawyer.
