## What's going on

- **The format admits its own joke.** The intro says they're jumping to hasty conclusions,
  "not what we normally do". That self-awareness earns trust, so the bold headings land as fun
  rather than clickbait.
- **Every section the same shape:** a bold verdict as the heading, then data, then a caveat or
  conditional ("if Hull don't improve soon"). Easy to scan.
- **Humour in small doses:** a bracketed aside mocking their own earlier hype, "even" used to
  deflate one team via a rival. Light and quick, never the point of the paragraph.
- **Graphics carry the evidence;** the text explains what the chart means in plain words.

## Steal-able

- A recurring series with a fixed shape, e.g. a weekly "knee-jerk" piece or thread from the
  Australian morning-after seat.
- Punchy verdict headings, softened by honest caveats in the body.
- The aside in brackets as his kind of humour.
