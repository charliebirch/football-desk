## What's going on

- **Sponsored content not labelled as such.** A casino appears at least four times, with abrupt
  pivots into ad copy, then a gambling notice right after the most moving line. It breaks trust
  and the mood.
- **The missing heart.** No writer's own life in Australia: no city, no friends, no group.
  The real loss (the ground, the shared ritual, being in the same day as the match) is
  gestured at, not explored. Its best moment is a personal one: conceding in the 94th minute
  and staring at the ceiling until sunrise. That supports Charlie's point: more of that would
  have made it.
- **Stock jokes.** The VAR line is the joke everyone makes about VAR; it could sit in any
  article. Charlie reads that as cheap and as "could have been written by AI".
- **Useful facts done basically.** The kick-off table is correct for the time zones but plain,
  and misses the weeks when only one country's clocks have changed.

## Steal-able (what to beat)

- Charlie's version of this piece is already in the inbox ("the 3am fan"). The gap: real
  emotion, his own life, no sponsors, a proper kick-off guide.
- The "could AI have written this?" test: if a line could appear in anyone's article, cut it.
