## What Charlie said

- I like that Adam's own personal opinions don't come across

- The title of the story isn't misleading or click baity, tells the story, gets to the point,
  and the reader is educated on the topic with all available current information

## What's going on

- **Straight news discipline.** The writer's view is kept out entirely; the authority comes
  from facts and attribution, not voice. Charlie noticed the absence of opinion as a positive.
- **Headline as a promise kept.** A news headline that is a plain statement of the event. The
  reader gets exactly what the title said. This became one of Charlie's recurring tests (see
  #2 and #14).
- **"All available current information".** A good news report leaves the reader up to date,
  nothing more. Length is set by what's known, not by a target.
