## What's going on

A podcast, not writing, so this is about format and angle rather than prose. Claude hasn't
heard the episode; this is from the episode listing and should be checked against Charlie's
answer.

- **A roundup of several stories in one sitting**, across sports: City's sponsor problem,
  Celtic, an NBA sanction, golf franchise valuations.
- **The business angle on every story.** The headline event is the way in; the money and
  power behind it is the point. The show's other format is long interviews with owners and
  operators, which feeds the news segment's knowledge.
- **A named, recurring segment** ("The Breakdown", numbered episodes): readers and listeners
  know what they're getting each week.

## Steal-able

- A recurring newsletter segment with a fixed shape per story, e.g. what happened, the money
  behind it, what it means for fans.
- Lead each item with the business consequence rather than the result.

## What his answer adds

- The pattern is **simple explanation, then consequence**: what happened, said plainly, then
  what it means for the sport. Now craft candidate C14 in `_profile.md`.
- The gap he names, **insider knowledge**, is the same one as #3 (the Athletic's access). His
  route round it so far: aggregate what insiders have said on record, cited, and add the
  explanation and consequences. Their knowledge comes from careers in the business; his has to
  come from research and data, which fits the lane.
