## What's going on

- **The same story, two contracts with the reader.** Read straight after #1, this shows the
  difference between reporting and opinion. Charlie likes both, as long as the headline tells
  you which one you're getting.
- **Personality as the product.** In a column the writer's attitude is the point: here,
  contempt for power acting without consequence. Charlie enjoyed it *more* because of that.
- **Headline sets the feel.** The title signals the stance before the first line ("insinuates
  a negative connotation"). Tone matching between headline and body is the rule he drew.
- **Presentation matters to him.** A side note, but it recurs in #13, #14 and #15.
