## What's going on

- **Opens by challenging a received idea.** It starts with the familiar praise ("well run")
  and argues that the praise hides a low ceiling. The whole piece tests that claim.
- **A coined label organises everything.** "B club" is never formally defined but becomes the
  frame: check Brentford and Bournemouth briefly, make Brighton the case study, return to the
  label at the end.
- **Claim first, stat second.** Almost every stat (Gross's numbers, eleven scorers, the
  December and February records) comes straight after the claim it supports.
- **Shape.** About 900 words in ten paragraphs: short, abstract opening and closing around a
  detailed middle. The ending is a conditional verdict, not a summary.

## Steal-able

- Name the idea. A short label for your argument gives the reader a handle.
- The 900-word analysis column as a target length (Charlie: "a good length").
- Claim, then the number that proves it.
