# inbox/ - rules of engagement

The one place where mess is allowed. Charlie dumps anything here, in any form: a thought
mid-podcast, a link, a half-sentence, a voice-memo transcript. From his phone he types ideas
into the **Ideas inbox** tab of the playbook doc instead (see `capture-idea`); both count.

- **Append, never tidy.** Add new entries to the bottom of `dump.md` with a date line. Don't
  rewrite or reorder what's there.
- **Processing is a separate act.** Only when Charlie asks ("process the inbox") does
  `capture-idea` turn entries into `ideas/` files. Then mark each processed entry with
  `→ ideas/<slug>.md` or `→ not an idea (reason)`, rather than deleting it.
- **Not everything is an idea.** A link might belong in a story's `sources.md`, an outlet note
  in `outlets/`, a reading tip in `library/`. Route it there, and say where it went.
- Podcast notes: one entry per episode with show, episode title, timestamp and the thought.
