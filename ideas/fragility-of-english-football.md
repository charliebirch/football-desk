## The question

The fragility of football in England, even with all the money and power.

## Why Charlie

The richest league in the world sits on top of clubs that can be days from collapse. A fan
watching from abroad sees the Premier League's global gloss, and the clubs underneath it.

## Where to start

- Business of Sport, Ep141: the Torquay United rescue, a consortium of fans and businesspeople
  with ten days to save the club ([Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/business-of-sport/id1690597762))
- The Swiss Ramble, EFL Championship Finances 2016-25 (Sep 2026)
- Recent clubs in administration and the football regulator's remit (check current position)
- Fans who've been through a rescue
