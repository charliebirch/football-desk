# Library

The books and writers that feed your style guide and your story research. Upload books you own to the Football Desk project as files; in published work, quote only short passages with attribution.

## Books to load first

| Book | Author | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Game of Our Lives | David Goldblatt | "How important is football to the English?"; football and English society |
| Richer Than God | David Conn | "Are City fans in denial?"; City's ownership story |
| The Price of Football | Kieran Maguire | Reading club accounts; ownership and finance pieces |
| Fever Pitch | Nick Hornby | First-person fan voice; the far-away-fan pieces |
| Soccernomics | Simon Kuper and Stefan Szymanski | Making a data-led argument readable |
| Inverting the Pyramid | Jonathan Wilson | Tactical history and context |
| Zonal Marking | Michael Cox | Explaining tactics to a general reader |
